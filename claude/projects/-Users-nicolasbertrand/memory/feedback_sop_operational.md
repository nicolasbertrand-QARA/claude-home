SOPs must describe operational processes, not restate normative requirements.

**Why:** Saying "Theodo France shall document processes for establishing and validating competences (prEN 18286 §7.2.1 d)" is just the requirement with the company name prepended. It tells auditors nothing about HOW the company actually does it. A procedure must be actionable: who does what, when, using which tools, with what outputs.

**How to apply:** When translating a standard requirement into SOP content:
- Replace "shall" statements with concrete operational steps (who, what, when, how, where it's recorded)
- Reference actual tools, roles, and workflows used at the company (e.g. "Group Managers review competence matrices in TL011 during quarterly talent reviews")
- Describe inputs, activities, outputs, and responsibilities — not obligations
- If you don't know the operational detail, ask the user rather than defaulting to a requirement restatement
