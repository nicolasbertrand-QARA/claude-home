At Theodo, **CMO = Chief Marketing Officer**, not Chief Medical Officer.

**Why it matters:** In medical device QARA work, the role that approves Intended Purpose, signs Qualification statements, and validates clinical claims is typically called "Chief Medical Officer" or "Medical Director." At Doctolib (a HealthTech) the CMO acronym genuinely refers to the Chief Medical Officer. At Theodo (digital consultancy), CMO refers to marketing — using the acronym in a regulatory document creates a serious role confusion.

**How to apply:**
- For the medical authority role in Theodo regulatory documents, use **Medical Director** (generic regulatory term) or **Medical Lead** — never "CMO."
- When Theodo acts as legal manufacturer of an MDSW, the Medical Director may be an internal hire or an external advisor; this role is not in the canonical Tracks DB but is regulatorily required.
- When importing artifacts from medical device peer SOPs (Doctolib, etc.), check every "CMO" reference and convert.
