### CRITICAL: REAL-TIME PRICE VERIFICATION
For EVERY stock you plan to include in the final report, you MUST:
1. Search "[TICKER] stock price today" to get the CURRENT live price
2. Cross-verify by fetching a live quote page (e.g. https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/[TICKER] or search "finance [TICKER] quote")
3. NEVER rely on prices from articles, blog posts, or analysis pieces — these are often weeks or months old
4. If the price you found in an article differs significantly (>5%) from the live quote, USE THE LIVE QUOTE
5. For buy/sell levels: verify they make sense relative to the CURRENT price. Do NOT recommend buying at $125 if the stock currently trades at $180 — that is stale data
6. Include the date of the price quote in each stock card (e.g., "Current Price: $180 [~166€] as of Apr 28, 2026")

Then select the TOP 5 stocks for each timeframe:

### SHORT TERM (<1 month)
Criteria: imminent catalysts (earnings, FDA, product launch), technical breakout setups, unusual options activity, momentum, short squeeze potential (high short interest), positive earnings revisions. Minimum $500M market cap.

### MID TERM (<1 year)
Criteria: fundamental undervaluation (low P/E vs growth rate, PEG < 1.5), sector tailwinds, product cycle inflections, institutional accumulation patterns, analyst upgrade cycle starting, mean-reversion from oversold conditions. Minimum $1B market cap.

### LONG TERM (10 years)
Criteria: secular growth trends (AI, biotech, clean energy, digital infrastructure, aging population, space), wide competitive moats, strong revenue compounding (>15% CAGR potential), market leadership, visionary management, strong balance sheet. Minimum $5B market cap.

### For EACH stock (all 15), you MUST determine:
1. **Ticker & Company Name**
2. **Current Price** (from your research)
3. **Why Selected** — 2-3 sentence investment thesis
4. **Best Time to Buy** — Specific entry strategy: support level, pullback target, technical entry, or "at current levels around $X"
5. **Best Time to Sell** — Exit strategy: resistance target, catalyst date, price target, or "hold for 10+ years, consider trimming above $X"
6. **Sell Timeframe** — Specific expected holding period (e.g., "2-4 weeks", "6-9 months", "By May 15 (earnings)", "Hold 10+ years"). Be precise, not just "short term".
7. **Expected Variation** — Percentage upside and range (e.g., "+15% to +25% within 3 weeks")
8. **Key Risk** — Main downside risk in one sentence
9. **Sources** — At minimum 2 URLs that support the thesis

### CURRENCY RULE — MANDATORY

Use the current USD/EUR (or relevant currency/EUR) exchange rate — look it up at the start of your research with a web search for "USD EUR exchange rate today". Example: "$423 [~389€]", "$5B [~4.6B€]". This applies everywhere in the email: stock cards, market snapshot, methodology — every single dollar or foreign currency amount.

## PHASE 3.5: INGEST THE 15 NEWSLETTER PICKS INTO STOCKPULSE /picks

Before touching the existing portfolio, push the 15 newsletter picks to StockPulse so they appear in the /picks triage UI. Without this step, only picks that happen to overlap with the existing portfolio ever land in the database.

POST all 15 picks (short + mid + long) as a single batch to /api/cron/feed-recommendations using the SAME schema as Phase 4 Step 3 below. Use the same batchDate (today, YYYY-MM-DD). The endpoint does NOT dedupe against existing portfolio holdings, so always send all 15 — duplicates with portfolio refresh in Phase 4 are acceptable and expected for overlap tickers (the /picks UI shows them as PENDING for triage).

Example (one POST, 15 items in the recommendations array):
```
curl -s -X POST $STOCKPULSE_URL/api/cron/feed-recommendations \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ADMIN_SECRET" \
  -d '{
    "batchDate": "YYYY-MM-DD",
    "recommendations": [ {15 pick objects, same schema as Phase 4 Step 3} ]
  }'
```

Verify the response is `{"created": 15, ...}` before moving on. If created < 15, investigate before continuing.

## PHASE 4: UPDATE EXISTING PORTFOLIO ON STOCKPULSE

After ingesting the 15 newsletter picks, update the analysis on ALL stocks currently in the portfolio on the StockPulse platform.

### Step 1: Get the current portfolio

Run this bash command to get all active stocks:
```
curl -s $STOCKPULSE_URL/api/stocks
```

This returns a JSON array of all portfolio stocks with their tickers.

### Step 2: Research each portfolio stock

For EVERY stock in the portfolio (not just the 15 newsletter picks), do a quick analysis:
- Search "[TICKER] stock analysis [current month] [current year]"
- Search "[TICKER] price target consensus"
- Check current price, recent catalysts, any changed outlook
- Determine: updated thesis (2-3 sentences), buy target, sell target, range, stop loss, risk level, sell timeframe, and 2 source URLs

Skip ETFs and crypto (assetType "ETF" or "CRYPTO") — only analyze stocks.

### Step 3: POST updated recommendations

For each analyzed stock, POST to the StockPulse API:
```
curl -s -X POST $STOCKPULSE_URL/api/cron/feed-recommendations \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ADMIN_SECRET" \
  -d '{
    "batchDate": "YYYY-MM-DD",
    "recommendations": [
      {
        "ticker": "TICKER",
        "companyName": "Company Name",
        "horizon": "SHORT|MID|LONG",
        "currentPrice": 123.45,
        "currentPriceEur": 105.67,
        "thesis": "Updated thesis with latest catalysts...",
        "buyTarget": 110,
        "sellTarget": 150,
        "rangeHigh": 160,
        "rangeLow": 100,
        "stopLoss": 90,
        "riskLevel": "LOW|MEDIUM|HIGH",
        "sellTimeframe": "specific timeframe e.g. 6-12 months",
        "sources": ["https://source1.com", "https://source2.com"]
      }
    ]
  }'
```

The ADMIN_SECRET and STOCKPULSE_URL are available as environment variables.

If a stock was ALSO one of the 15 newsletter picks, include the newsletter analysis (it will be the freshest). For portfolio-only stocks not in the newsletter, create a standalone update.

IMPORTANT: This step is MANDATORY. Every Monday, the portfolio analysis must be refreshed. Do NOT skip this phase.

## ABSOLUTE RULES
1. Perform AT LEAST 20 distinct web searches — breadth of research is critical
2. Fetch AT LEAST 5 specific web pages for deeper analysis
3. Every stock pick MUST have at least 2 source URLs — NO EXCEPTIONS
4. The HTML file MUST be written to /tmp/stock_newsletter.html. Email delivery is the wrapper script's job, not yours.
5. Be SPECIFIC with buy/sell levels and price ranges — avoid vague advice
6. Include the disclaimer at the bottom of every email
7. NEVER fabricate sources, URLs, prices, or data — only report what you actually found
8. Do NOT recommend penny stocks (<$5), OTC stocks, or stocks with <$500M market cap for short/mid term
9. Long-term picks should have >$5B market cap and proven business models
10. If you find conflicting opinions on a stock, mention both bull and bear cases
11. Prioritize stocks where multiple independent sources converge on a bullish thesis
12. The research phase should take significant effort — do NOT cut corners
13. ALL PRICES MUST BE VERIFIED LIVE — search "[TICKER] stock price today" for every single pick. Article prices are often stale by weeks or months. If your buy/sell levels don't make sense relative to the current live price, you have stale data — fix it before including the stock
14. You ARE allowed to re-recommend stocks from previous newsletters if the analysis has changed — updated targets, new catalysts, revised risk, or shifted timeframe. When you do, note what changed (e.g. "Returning pick — raised target after Q1 beat"). Do NOT avoid a stock just because it was picked before; fresh analysis on the same stock is valuable.
